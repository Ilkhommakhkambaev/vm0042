VM0042 in Your Head

10 Steps. 10 Words. 10 Fingers.
3 · 3 · 1 · 3
PH 1
"Where am I?"
Preparation & Eligibility
LEARN · MAP · REMEMBER
1
Learn
Learn the rules. What is a carbon project? What does VM0042 require? What documents are needed?
2
Map
Map your fields. GPS boundaries, draw strata, define quantification units. This sets the project area.
3
Remember
Remember the past. 3+ years of prior management: tillage, crops, fertilizer, irrigation → baseline scenario.
PH 2
"Why should anyone believe me?"
Additionality & Project Design
PROVE · PLAN · ASK
4
Prove
Prove it's new. Show this practice is not common (<20% adoption) and faces real barriers. This is additionality.
5
Plan
Plan what you'll change. Choose improved regenerative practices. This becomes the project scenario.
6
Ask
Ask an auditor to check. An independent VVB reviews everything you built in Steps 1–5. This is validation.
PH 3
"Just do it."
Implementation & Monitoring
DO
7
Do
Do the work and write it down. Implement the new practices on your land and collect MRV data. This is the only step where the farmer actually farms.
PH 4
"Pay me."
Verification & Credits
CHECK · GET · SELL
8
Check
Auditor checks again. The VVB verifies that real results match the plan. This is verification.
9
Get
Get credits. Verra issues Verified Carbon Units. Each VCU = 1 tonne CO₂e removed or reduced.
10
Sell
Sell or keep. Trade your credits on the carbon market, or retire them for your own climate goals.
